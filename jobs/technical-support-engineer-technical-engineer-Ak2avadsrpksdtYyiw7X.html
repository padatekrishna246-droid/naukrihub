Job Description

Procigma is seeking a technically skilled engineer to join our Technical Support Team. This role involves managing product selection, providing technical support, performing calculations, conducting customer meetings, and resolving technical issues. Key Responsibilities: - Selecting products based on specific customer applications. - Providing technical support to customers. - Conducting technical meetings. - Performing technical calculations. - Resolving customer technical issues. - Supporting technical seminars. - Participating in exhibitions. - Managing technical activities and addressing customer inquiries. Required Skills & Experience: - 3-5 years of experience in Machine Design or Product Design. - Knowledge of product selection and applications. - Proficiency with SolidWorks or other 3D CAD software. - Solid understanding of Mathematics and Physics. - Strong fundamentals in Mechanical Engineering. Additional Requirements: - Good verbal and written communication skills. - Willingness to travel across India. - Willingness to relocate if required. - A long-term career-oriented mindset.
