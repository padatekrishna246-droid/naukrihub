Job Description

CommScope is looking for a Technician I, Quality to support quality activities at its Verna, Goa facility. The role involves supporting process and product quality activities, inspecting and auditing manufacturing processes, identifying defects, analyzing quality issues, and supporting corrective and preventive actions. Key Responsibilities: • Support process and product auditing activities. • Interpret engineering drawings and related specifications. • Identify and analyze product and process defects. • Apply basic quality tools, including the 7 QC tools. • Support root cause analysis (RCA) and 8D problem-solving activities. • Assist in identifying quality issues and implementing corrective actions. • Maintain quality standards and support continuous improvement activities. • Work independently with minimal supervision. • Maintain accurate quality-related records and documentation. • Follow company quality, safety and operational procedures. Required Qualifications: • Diploma qualification. • Preferably 3+ years of relevant experience. • Good verbal and written communication skills. • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office. • Good knowledge of engineering drawings and related specifications. • Knowledge of process and product auditing. • Defect analysis skills. • Basic knowledge of the 7 QC tools. • Knowledge of Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and 8D. • Good attendance and work record. • Ability to work independently with little or no supervision.
