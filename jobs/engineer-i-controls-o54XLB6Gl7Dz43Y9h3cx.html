CommScope

Job Description

CommScope is seeking an Engineer I, Controls in Verna, Goa, to lead industrial controls, automation projects, system troubleshooting, and equipment commissioning in manufacturing operations. Key Responsibilities: • Lead controls for capital expenditure and automation projects from concept to commissioning. • Support automation installations and ensure control systems align with operational requirements. • Program and troubleshoot PLCs (Allen-Bradley, Siemens, Delta, Mitsubishi), VFDs, servos, HMIs, and SCADA systems. • Maintain electrical schematics, panel layouts, system architectures, and Bills of Materials (BOM). • Diagnose industrial communication issues (Ethernet/IP, Profinet, Modbus) and electromechanical control systems. • Perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) to improve machine uptime. • Coordinate with external integrators and vendors for new production line installations. • Comply with safety requirements, continuous improvement initiatives, and 5S standards. Qualifications & Skills: • Bachelor's or Diploma in Electrical, Electronics, Controls, Instrumentation Engineering, or a related field. • 5–7 years of experience in maintenance, automation, or controls within a manufacturing environment. • Expertise in electromechanical troubleshooting, PLC/HMI/SCADA systems, and industrial communication protocols. • Familiarity with power distribution systems, control system drawings, and basic hydraulic/pneumatic troubleshooting. • Cable manufacturing experience and knowledge of Statistical Process Control (SPC) are advantageous.
