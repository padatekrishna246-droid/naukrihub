Job Description

ob Description: CommScope is looking for an Engineer I, Controls to support manufacturing and industrial automation activities at its Verna, Goa facility. The role involves troubleshooting and maintaining electromechanical control systems and working with PLC, VFD, servo, HMI and SCADA systems. Responsibilities: Troubleshoot electromechanical control systems used in continuous manufacturing equipment. Program and troubleshoot PLC systems including Allen-Bradley, Siemens, Delta and Mitsubishi. Program, tune, commission and troubleshoot VFD and servo systems. Develop and troubleshoot HMI and SCADA systems. Diagnose industrial communication systems including Ethernet/IP, Profinet and Modbus. Work with temperature controllers using PID and ON/OFF control. Troubleshoot electrical power systems including transformers, motors, DG systems, circuit breakers and distribution systems. Perform RCA and FMEA activities. Support hydraulic and pneumatic system troubleshooting. Read and understand power and control system drawings. Maintain workplace 5S standards. Requirements: Bachelor’s or Diploma degree in Electrical, Electronics, Controls & Instrumentation Engineering or a related field. Experience with industrial PLC programming and troubleshooting. Knowledge of VFD, servo, HMI and SCADA systems. Knowledge of industrial communication protocols. Understanding of electrical power and control systems. Troubleshooting and analytical skills.
